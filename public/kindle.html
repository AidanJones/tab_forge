Using this on your Kindle

Keep the laptop server running and connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi. Open this page using the laptop’s network address; 127.0.0.1 on the Kindle points to the Kindle itself.

On your laptop, run pnpm dev:lan, then open the Network URL ending in /kindle.html. Use a built production site for older browsers that cannot load the normal development app.

The reader uses static diagrams and page turns. Use the full app on your laptop for audio, microphone note checking, scrolling play-along, USB MIDI, editing and the 3D hand. API availability varies by Kindle firmware; these features have not been verified on your device.

To share a score with the Kindle, put its Guitar Pro, MusicXML or alphaTex file in public/scores on the laptop, then choose Scores → Refresh list. Scores saved only in the laptop browser are not automatically available on the Kindle. Download a saved score from its project details first.

Notes and bookmarks here stay in this browser and are separate from your laptop projects. Keep another copy of anything important.
